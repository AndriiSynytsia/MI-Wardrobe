About MI Wardrobe

Reimagining daily dressing through intelligent design.

MI Wardrobe began with a simple belief: everyday furniture should serve your life effortlessly. Engineered in Warsaw, Poland, our modular smart closet blends silent mechanics, intuitive touch controls, and refined cabinetry to bring order and calm to your home.

Warsaw, Poland Dedicated Engineering Team Tailored Dressing Systems
01 / Our Story

Craftsmanship & Engineering

We bring together specialists in mechanical engineering, physical computing, and furniture design. Every MI Wardrobe combines dual motorized conveyor loops, optical garment registration, and a dedicated capacitive interface designed to blend seamlessly into luxury residences.

02 / Philosophy

Quiet Precision in Every Detail

We believe smart home technology should feel calm, transparent, and genuinely practical. Rather than adding complexity, MI Wardrobe focuses on mechanical reliability and seamless interaction—bringing the garments you choose directly to your reach with whisper-quiet motion.

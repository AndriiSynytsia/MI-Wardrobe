About MI Wardrobe

Reimagining daily storage through automation.

MI Wardrobe began as an ambitious engineering challenge: how do we transform a static, everyday piece of furniture into an intelligent, automated assistant? Founded by four computer engineering students in Warsaw, Poland, the project bridges mechanical automation, embedded systems, and intuitive interface design to solve a universal daily routine—organizing clothes and choosing what to wear.

Warsaw, Poland 4 co-founders University prototype
01 / Team

The team behind the prototype

We are a team of four co-founders bringing together distinct disciplines across software architecture, physical computing, product visualization, and user experience. What started as collaborative academic coursework evolved into a complete working hardware prototype—combining dual motorized conveyor carousels, integrated barcode garment registration, and a dedicated console interface.

02 / Philosophy

Our philosophy

We believe smart home technology should feel calm, transparent, and genuinely practical. Rather than adding unnecessary complexity, MI Wardrobe focuses on mechanical reliability and seamless interaction—bringing the garments you choose directly to your reach. As a university prototype, it stands as proof of concept for the future of connected residential living.

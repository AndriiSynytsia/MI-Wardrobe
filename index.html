01

Deterministic Optical Indexing

Every garment carrier locks into an indexed slot on the upper or lower 6063-T6 track via a 650 nm optical barcode scan—stored strictly in an encrypted local SQLite vault.

02

Local-First Curation & Silhouette Staging

Compose multi-piece ensembles on the 30 cm anti-glare IPS display. Zero cameras are installed in the bezel; all visual staging runs locally on the embedded processor.

03

Dual-Loop Shortest-Path Delivery

Closed-loop rear stepper drives compute optimal clockwise/counter-clockwise rotation, delivering both upper and lower garments simultaneously to the 3500K illuminated bay.

Commissioning & Garment Registration Protocol [ Technical Protocol + ]
  1. Step 01
    Hardware Cryptographic Pairing: Pair your local device to the wardrobe's internal controller via one-time physical serial handshake.
  2. Step 02
    650 nm Optical Hanger Lock: Engage each garment on its precision carrier, scan the retail or MI tag, and assign to an indexed slot coordinate.
  3. Step 03
    Optional Silhouette Calibration: Upload a static reference photo for 2D/3D ensemble overlay (processed locally; zero biometric camera hardware).
  4. Step 04
    Dynamic Load Balancing: MI OS automatically distributes heavy overcoats and suits across the upper loop to minimize stepper torque variance.

Privacy by Hardware Architecture

MI Wardrobe is engineered from the physical schematic upward for zero-surveillance domestic privacy. The flush-mounted 30 cm capacitive IPS console contains zero optical camera sensors and zero ambient microphones.

All garment metadata, 650 nm barcode indexes, and ensemble staging profiles reside inside an encrypted local SQLite vault on the wardrobe's internal controller bus.

When you submit a commissioning or architectural enquiry, your details are used exclusively by our Warsaw engineering desk to coordinate delivery or CAD/BIM dispatch.

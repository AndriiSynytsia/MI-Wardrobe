02 / Mechanical Architecture

See what happens behind the doors.

A calm, precision separation revealing two automated conveyor levels, capacitive touch column, and optical garment identification.

Exploded View: 0% · Rails in motion
Assembled View Scroll to reveal internal engineering.
01 / Front Panels & Doors
Bi-parting protective panels in warm lacquered finish with left screen mount.
02 / Capacitive Screen (≈30 cm)
Built-in display for digital wardrobe browsing & outfit preview ritual.
03 / Outer Shell & Top Power Bus
Top architrave housing master gantry power supply and interior LED rails.
04 / Upper Rail Conveyor
Continuous oval loop for dresses, suits, and long coats (~40 positions).
05 / Lower Rail Conveyor
Independent medium-height loop for shirts and trousers (~20 positions).
06 / Hangers & Barcode Scanner
Automated hanger engagement with optical registration.
07 / Collection Opening (≈32 cm)
Central dual-tier illuminated aperture presenting selected items.
08 / Rear Stepper Motor & Unit
Center-rear drive motor controlling smooth continuous carousel rotation.
09 / Plinth & Adjustable Feet
Heavy structural plinth with vibration dampening and leveling feet.

First-time setup workflow [ Expand + ]
  1. Step 1
    Connect & Create Account: Set up a secure user account via mobile pairing.
  2. Step 2
    Register Clothes One by One: Hang item on smart hanger lock, scan or type barcode, and fetch product details from store page.
  3. Step 3
    Add Personal Photo: Upload a standing photo to enable the outfit preview concept (visualisation only; no body measurements).
  4. Step 4
    Review & Finish: Verify rail assignments (upper rail for long pieces, lower rail for tops/trousers).

About the Prototype

MI Wardrobe was conceived to rethink everyday dressing rituals. By uniting dual-tier motorized garment circulation with a capacitive collection assistant, garments are brought within effortless reach.

The 3D presentation on this page is generated procedurally in WebGL (Three.js), demonstrating the exact separation axes of internal assemblies.

Material Specification & Craft

Tactile furniture finishes. Industrial-grade mechanics.

Every surface of MI Wardrobe is selected to balance quiet residential warmth on the outside with durable, low-friction mechanical precision inside the dual-carousel cavity.

01 / Exterior Shell & Fascia

Matte White & Deep Matte Black

The outer structural panels feature a seamless, non-reflective architectural matte white finish with zero wood grain distraction, paired with a deep neutral matte black upper power fascia, plinth, and aperture framing.

Surface Roughness0.80 – 0.88 (Soft Matte)
Panel CoreHigh-density furniture MDF
02 / Interior Cavity

Natural Dark Walnut Veneer

All internal furniture surfaces—including the rear acoustic panel, internal floor, side walls, and ceiling—are finished in rich, warm dark walnut with natural vertical wood grain and a satin-matte protective seal.

Finish TypeSatin-matte natural walnut
Grain OrientationContinuous vertical bookmatch
03 / Dual Carousel Hardware

Brushed Aluminium & Machined Steel

The upper and lower racetrack conveyor loops combine brushed anodised aluminium guide rails, CNC-machined steel ceiling collars, and powder-coated structural support columns engineered for quiet continuous rotation.

Conveyor Rails6063-T6 Anodised Aluminium
Support ColumnsPowder-coated structural steel
04 / Aperture & Hangers

Frosted Hazy Glass & Smart Hangers

Two central collection doors use lightly diffused hazy safety glass that softens the view of stored garments while glowing warmly when an outfit arrives. Custom hangers pair solid beechwood, matte ABS, and chrome hooks.

Aperture DoorsTempered frosted safety glass
Garment HangersBeechwood, matte ABS & chrome
Integrated Illumination

3500K Warm-Neutral Architectural LED

Recessed interior lighting is tuned to approximately 3500K warm-neutral white with high colour rendering (CRI > 95), ensuring natural fabric colours and walnut tones look true to life without glare or harsh shadows.

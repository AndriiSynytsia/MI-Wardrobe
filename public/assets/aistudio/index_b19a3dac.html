About this prototype

MI Wardrobe is presented as a university product concept. The wardrobe image and interface recording were supplied for this project.

The exploded view moves regions of the original image. It is an image-based motion study, with no reconstructed internal geometry or claim of photorealistic 3D simulation.

The assistant responses are illustrative. Model capacity, pricing, venue details and final feature availability require confirmation. This page does not accept orders.

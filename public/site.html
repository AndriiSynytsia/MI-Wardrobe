Privacy by Hardware Architecture

MI Wardrobe is engineered from the physical schematic upward for zero-surveillance domestic privacy. The flush-mounted 30 cm capacitive IPS console contains zero optical camera sensors and zero ambient microphones.

All garment metadata, 650 nm barcode indexes, and ensemble staging profiles reside inside an encrypted local SQLite vault on the wardrobe's internal controller bus.

When you submit a commissioning or architectural enquiry, your details are used exclusively by our Warsaw engineering desk to coordinate delivery or CAD/BIM dispatch.

Hardware Commissioning & Specification Terms

MI Wardrobe systems (Series MI-01 Mini and Series MI-02 Pro) are precision-manufactured to order in Warsaw. Dimensional tolerances, electrical rough-in requirements (230V AC 50Hz / 24V DC internal bus), and millwork clearances are governed by the official CAD/BIM Revision 2026.4 specification package.

Pre-order reservations lock in priority production batch allocation and introductory pricing without immediate payment obligation until final architectural sign-off.

About Mi Wardrobe

Warsaw Mechatronics & Engineering Pedigree

MI Wardrobe bridges enterprise-grade automated storage and retrieval systems (AS/RS) with residential luxury joinery. By uniting dual-tier 6063-T6 anodised aluminium carousels, closed-loop NEMA stepper drives, and an air-gapped capacitive MI OS console, up to 90 garments are indexed and delivered in under 4.2 seconds at < 31 dBA.

The interactive WebGL 3D twin on this page is driven directly by our production CAD assembly, allowing architects and clients to inspect every sub-assembly in real time.
